<objective>
**As a** lifter who took six weeks off for a broken wrist and came back nervous, **I want to** open the app and
be told the same weight I was on, **so that** the app is not silently punishing me for living a life outside
the gym.

This is the phase's most consequential negative-case plan, and the one requirement that is easiest to break
by accident: PRGR-08 forbids a reduced recommendation as a consequence of missing sessions, and almost
every natural implementation of "recency" reaches for elapsed time and violates it. D-10 answers it
structurally — the engine reads a positional list of logged sessions and has no clock — and this plan makes
that answer testable rather than merely intended.

It also lands the two numbers this phase invents. D-05's three-consecutive-shortfall threshold and D-06's
one-rep tolerance band have **no public source**: research confirmed that, and confirmed that the closest
published autoregulation model operates on a different axis entirely (reps missed within a single set, not
shortfalls across consecutive sessions). Both constants, their comments and any copy this plan writes must
say so plainly rather than implying a provenance that does not exist.

Purpose: make falling short hold, make missing sessions inert, and make imprecise reps-in-reserve harmless.
Output: PRGR-08, PRGR-09 and PRGR-10, each with a named constant and a rendered consequence.
</objective>
