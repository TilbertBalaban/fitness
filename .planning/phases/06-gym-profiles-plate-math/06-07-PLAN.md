<objective>
Let a user change which gym the current workout is happening at, without discarding the workout and
without disturbing anything already logged.

Purpose: GYM-04's "switch gyms mid-program" is already satisfied by 06-01's stamp-at-start — the
next session simply starts under whichever gym is active. What is missing is the real case the
decision names: you arrived, and it is not the gym you assumed. This plan makes that a two-tap
correction rather than a reason to throw the session away.

Output: `SwitchGymSheet`, the session menu's fourth row, and a real-browser proof that a restamp
moves forward-looking resolution while leaving logged sets alone.
</objective>
