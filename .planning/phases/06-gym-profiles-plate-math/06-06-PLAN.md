Close GYM-07: the rack is taken, or the machine is broken, and the app both records that for this
workout and offers exercises the gym can still equip — without silently editing the gym profile.

Purpose: this is the last consumer of D-21's resolved inventory and the one that proves the
abstraction was worth having. Marking a machine unavailable must simultaneously collapse its band,
remove it from the achievable set, and drop it from substitute candidates — three behaviours that
could only stay in agreement by reading one subtracted view.

Output: the session-scoped unavailability column and its live schema push, the two-tier
availability sheet, equipment-aware substitutes through the existing swap mechanism, and the fix
for the long-standing swap write-path defect this is the first plan to actually exercise.
