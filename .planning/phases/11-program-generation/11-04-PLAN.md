<objective>
**As a** lifter who trains four days a week and prefers an upper/lower rotation, **I want to** pick
that split explicitly — or let the app pick the one that fits my days — **so that** the generated
week matches how I actually train rather than a shape someone else assumed.

11-01 landed the split table's SHAPE and its resolution contract with `full_body` entries populated.
This plan fills the rest of the table: `upper_lower` and `push_pull_legs` across every supported day
count, the `auto` mapping for 2 through 6 days, and the explicit declaration of the three pairs this
project deliberately does not support. It changes no exported signature, so `generate.ts` — 11-01's
sole-owned file for the whole phase — is untouched.

It also lands the contract test the assumption-delta checkpoint called for: a preference added to
`SPLIT_PREFERENCES` later must not be able to resolve silently to nothing.

Deliberately NOT in this plan: emphasis, session-length trimming, deloads and the degradation
collector (all 11-01), the exclusion wiring (11-03), the wizard (11-05), and the parity and
durability proofs (11-06).

Purpose: make every split a user can choose a real, declared, diffable week.
Output: a complete split table plus an invariant that keeps it complete.
</objective>
