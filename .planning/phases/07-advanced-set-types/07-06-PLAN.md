<objective>
**As a** lifter alternating two exercises, **I want to** pair them with one tap from the exercise's own
action sheet and unpair them just as easily, **so that** the app stops trying to rest me between the two
halves of a superset I am doing back to back.

Purpose: `session_exercise.superset_group_id` has existed since Phase 4 and has never been written by
anything — `days.ts:148` and `duplicate-routine.ts:99` both carry comments marking the gap. This plan is
its first real writer, session-scoped only (D-16). It also lands the one predicate every superset
behaviour in 07-07 reads: "is this exercise the final member of its own group", computed over LIVE
members and scoped to the members sharing this exercise's own group id — never `order_index` read
globally, which is Pitfall 4's exact failure. D-24 falls out of that predicate for free: a group whose
membership has shrunk to one live exercise behaves like no group at all.

Output: SETS-07's and SETS-08's data and action-sheet halves, plus the two per-side rows appended in the
same additive change to the same closed union (their behaviour is 07-08's).
</objective>
