<objective>
**As a** lifter scrolling back through months of workouts, **I want to** see the shape of my training above
the list rather than only the individual sessions, **so that** I can tell at a glance whether the last three
months went up or down.

This plan ships S2: the History trend card, its batched local read, and executed browser evidence. It
consumes the `historyTrendSeries` aggregation 09-02 landed and the `TrendChart` and `SegmentedChipRow`
09-01 landed, and adds no computation of its own beyond assembling inputs and formatting output.

Deliberately NOT in this plan: the Home tab's card (09-04), the Records screen and the History header's
Records link (09-03, previous wave — this plan inherits that edit and must not alter it), and the
performance screen's ranges (09-06).

Purpose: put the shape of the training year above the list of individual days, without ever drawing a week
that did not happen.
Output: a working Trends card on the History tab with every UI-SPEC state and real browser evidence.
</objective>
