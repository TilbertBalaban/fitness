<objective>
**As a** lifter who wants to know whether I am doing enough, **I want to** see this week's sets, exercises
and muscle groups against what my program asks for, the moment I finish logging and before anything syncs,
**so that** I can tell a light week from a full one without doing arithmetic.

This plan ships S1: the Last 7 Days card on the Home tab, its batched local read, and executed browser
evidence. It consumes the pure `weeklyProgress` aggregation 09-02 already landed and adds no computation of
its own beyond assembling that function's inputs.

Deliberately NOT in this plan: the History tab's trend card (09-05), the Records screen (09-03), the
performance screen's ranges (09-06), and any live or watched query. 09-RESEARCH §4 pins that success
criterion 4's "before any sync" clause is about LOCALITY — the figure is derived from local SQLite with no
server round-trip — and not about same-screen live updating. Returning from the workout screen re-focuses
Home, which re-reads and shows the new numbers. That satisfies the criterion on the existing idiom with no
new infrastructure, and the Home screen already carries a comment naming the live-update gap as known and
deliberate; this phase must not silently close it in one card while four other screens keep the old
behaviour.

Purpose: answer "am I doing enough this week" on-device, honestly, including when the answer is "you have
not trained".
Output: a working Last 7 Days card with every UI-SPEC state and real browser evidence.
</objective>
