<objective>
Give the Reorder row an actual interaction.

`reorderSessionExercises` is implemented and unit-tested (contiguous `order_index` over non-removed
rows), but `SessionActionSheet`'s Reorder row is a documented no-op because no drag surface was
defined anywhere in `05-UI-SPEC.md` — E10 lists Reorder as one of four fixed rows and specifies no
interaction for it (WINDOWS #116). 05-13 authored that contract (`Amendment A.3`, `E12`); this plan
builds it.

The surface is grounded entirely in what already ships: Phase 4's `DragHandle` + `DragHandle.web`
pair, its pure `reorder-drag.ts` drop arithmetic, and `SessionActionSheet`/`RemoveExerciseDialog`'s
overlay card shape. Nothing new is invented beyond the sheet that composes them.

Purpose: LOG-14's reorder half stops being a mutation with no way to reach it.
Output: a `ReorderExercisesSheet`, a transactional `reorderSessionExercises`, and a browser-real
spec that drags a row and asserts the committed order.
</objective>
