<objective>
**As a** person training, **I want to** log a whole workout — every exercise, every set — with my phone in
airplane mode and have all of it arrive intact, **so that** the app can hold what I actually did rather
than a shape that only fits toy data.

Plan 02-01 proved one row through every layer. This plan widens that slice from one row to the whole
session aggregate, and lands the domain model the rest of the project is downstream of.
`ARCHITECTURE.md` §1 opens by saying retrofitting this model once real history exists is expensive — that
sentence is the reason this phase exists, and this is the plan where it is honoured or not.

Two decisions become columns here. `local_date` and `timezone` on `workout_session` (LOG-22) make "which
day was that workout" a stored fact rather than a re-derivation against whatever clock is reading it.
`weight_kg` as a decimal rather than a float (D-04) makes drift impossible by column type rather than by
convention.

Scope note: this plan lands the *shape* of the whole catalogue — `muscle_group`, `exercise`,
`exercise_muscle_mapping` — but not its content. Sourcing, normalising and licensing ~900 exercises, and
every screen that browses them, remains Phase 3. `02-RESEARCH.md` Decision 4 is the reasoning: the seeded
performance corpus in plan 02-07 cannot be realistic against a stub schema, and under-modelling now is the
expensive direction.

Purpose: PLAT-02 at full-workout scale, PLAT-07's durability substrate, and LOG-22.
Output: the domain schema, in Postgres and mirrored locally, with a whole workout flowing through it.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions (probe-surfaced, unresolved)

The deterministic edge probe classified **LOG-22** as `unclassified` and left it `unresolved`. An
unclassified row is never auto-resolved, so it is surfaced here rather than dropped:

> **LOG-22 — "User's workout is attributed to the calendar day it was logged in, regardless of timezone or
> a late-night finish."** The probe could not assign an edge category, so no boundary predicate was derived
> automatically. The planner's reading is that its real edge axis is **which instant and whose clock**, and
> both halves are answered by stored columns rather than by a rule: the instant is `started_at`, and the
> clock is the IANA zone captured on the device at that instant. The midnight boundary, the DST boundary
> and the travel case are covered as explicit truths and by `calendar-day.test.ts`. **Assumption:** no
> additional unlisted edge exists on this requirement.

Two decisions in this plan are `02-RESEARCH.md` assumptions rather than verified external requirements, and
are recorded rather than hidden:

- **A3 — attributing the day from `started_at` rather than `ended_at`.** Reasoned, not verified: it is the
  intuitive answer to "which day did I train", and it stops a session's day flipping mid-workout while it
  is still being logged. It is kept recoverable by storing `started_at`, `ended_at` and `timezone`
  separately, so a later change of policy is a recomputation rather than lost information.
- **Decision 4 — landing the full schema now while seeding only a representative exercise fixture.** If
  the developer would rather Phase 2 land only the session tables, say so before plan 02-07 generates the
  corpus against these shapes.
