<objective>
**As a** person who has been logging training for months, **I want to** take my data out of the app and
**I want to** be warned before I sign out with a workout still stuck on the device, **so that** my training
log is mine and I cannot lose it by accident.

Two loose ends close here.

The first is Phase 1's deliberate stub. `sign-out.ts` shipped `pendingWriteCount()` returning a hard-coded
zero, with a comment saying Phase 2 would replace the body rather than thread a confirmation into a
lifecycle that had already shipped without one. That seam exists precisely so this plan is a one-function
change instead of a redesign. Take it as it was left — keep the `getPendingCount` injection point, replace
what it defaults to.

The second is PLAT-10. `02-RESEARCH.md` Decision 7 settles it as a client-side JSON export read straight
from local SQLite: the only version that works in the gym, and the only one that includes writes that have
not synced yet. A server-side export would see the fully merged cross-device history and is a natural
addition once Phase 9 and 10's server analytics exist — it is deferred, not rejected, and `02-CONTEXT.md`
is explicit that this is one requirement and not a subsystem.

The one thing worth being careful about is honesty. An export that quietly omits something is worse than no
export, because the person will believe they have a backup. The document carries a header stating what it
holds and what it could not reach.

Purpose: PLAT-10 and D-08.
Output: a file the person owns, and a confirmation that finally knows what it is warning about.
</objective>
