<objective>
**As a** lifter who changes my program between blocks, **I want to** edit it freely without any
already-logged workout changing under me, **so that** my history stays a record of what I actually
did rather than a projection of what my program currently says.

Purpose: snapshot-on-use is the mechanism that makes success criterion 4 true, and it already
exists (D-01). PROG-11 is therefore already satisfied by construction for the base prescription —
`session_exercise` carries six frozen `target_*` columns copied once at session start, and
`routine_exercise_id` is a traceability column with no foreign key. But 04-07 introduced a second
source of truth for a prescription, and `apps/mobile/lib/db/log-set.ts`'s `addSessionExercise`
still reads `routine_exercise`'s base columns alone. The moment any program has a per-cycle
override, every session logged against it snapshots the wrong numbers — with no error, no failing
test and no visible symptom until someone notices the weights in their history do not match what the
builder showed them.

This plan closes that gap and turns "editing never corrupts history" from an architectural claim into
an asserted regression, on both the client and the server.

Output: a session snapshot that resolves the cycle's targets, and tests that fail if a future change
ever makes a logged session re-read the routine.
</objective>
