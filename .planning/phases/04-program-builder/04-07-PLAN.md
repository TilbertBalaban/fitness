<objective>
**As a** lifter whose third week is heavier than the first two, **I want to** change just the numbers
that differ in that cycle, **so that** one heavier week is one small edit rather than a second copy of
my whole program.

Purpose: PROG-04 says cycles each have "their own targets". The way to satisfy that literally without
violating the architecture's ban on per-week duplication (D-02) is a **sparse override table**:
`routine_exercise` keeps its six columns as the single mutable base prescription, and
`routine_exercise_cycle_target` holds a row only where a value actually differs, resolved as
`override ?? base` per field.

This plan also builds the one function that resolution runs through. Three consumers need it — the
builder's cycle strip, the Home tab's next-up card, and `log-set.ts`'s session snapshot — and if any
of them reimplements it, the numbers the session freezes can disagree with the numbers the builder
showed, silently and forever.

And it is the deepest ownership chain in the codebase: an override row hangs off **two** parents that
must independently resolve to the same routine.

Output: per-cycle targets that sync, and one shared resolver.
</objective>
