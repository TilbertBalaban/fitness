<objective>
**As a** lifter running a mesocycle, **I want to** organise my program into cycles, drop a deload at
the start or the end, and schedule time off, **so that** the shape of my block is something the app
holds rather than something I keep in my head.

Purpose: PROG-04, PROG-05 and PROG-06 have no home in the shipped schema — there is no cycle table,
no deload column and no time-off column. D-09 makes a cycle a first-class row, D-12 makes deload and
time off *kinds* of cycle rather than separate columns, and D-13 requires the vocabulary to be a
single exported tuple enforced on both sides, because `PITFALLS.md` §9 is exactly the cost of leaving
a discriminator as free text and Phase 3 already paid that cost once with `load_type`.

This plan builds the table, the vocabulary and its enforcement, the pull-side sync query and the
push-side apply path. Authoring cycles in the UI is 04-08; per-cycle target overrides are 04-07.

Output: cycles that exist, sync both directions, and cannot hold a value outside the vocabulary.
</objective>

<flagged_assumptions>
The spec-less edge probe returned `unclassified` for PROG-05 and PROG-06 and could not propose a
shape category for either. Both are carried here as explicit planner assumptions rather than
auto-resolved:

- **A-PROG-05 (unresolved).** "Place a deload at the start or end of a cycle" is read as *at the
  start or end of the program's cycle sequence* — a `deload` cycle at the lowest or the highest
  `order_index` — matching `FEATURES.md` line 40's "Deload First Cycle" / "Deload Last Cycle" pair.
  The alternative reading, a deload placed at the start or end of *an individual cycle* (that is,
  certain days within a week being deload days), is not supported by this model and would need a
  per-day kind rather than a per-cycle one. This plan further assumes a deload is **trained** — a
  lighter week you still perform — rather than a rest period, which is why it is a separate kind
  from `time_off` and why 04-10's rotation counts it. If a deload was meant to be a sub-range of
  days inside a cycle, the `kind` column is on the wrong table.

- **A-PROG-06 (unresolved).** "Schedule planned time off within a program" is read as a `time_off`
  cycle occupying a position in the cycle sequence with a duration in whole days, measured from the
  last completed session. It is deliberately **not** calendar-bound: D-19 made the program a
  floating sequence, so time off is "seven days from when you stop", not "the week of the 14th".
  This plan also assumes time off is scheduled between cycles rather than mid-cycle — a break in the
  middle of a training week would need a position finer than `order_index` over cycles. If planned
  time off was meant to carry real dates, or to interrupt a cycle rather than sit between cycles,
  this model cannot express it and a calendar binding would be an additive layer (already recorded
  as deferred in CONTEXT.md).
</flagged_assumptions>
