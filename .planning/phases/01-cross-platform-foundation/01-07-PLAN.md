<objective>
Build the navigation shell D-09 specifies: one Expo Router route tree that renders OS-native tabs on iOS and
Android and a real, deep-linkable tab bar in a desktop browser, with Home, Programs, Workout, History, and
Profile as five labelled screens.

D-09 is deliberately more than the roadmap's success criterion strictly requires, and the reasoning matters
for how this is built: a single screen exercises almost none of Expo Router, and "React Native Web
divergence discovered late" is a named project pitfall. A five-tab scaffold with two platform-specific
layouts forces that divergence into the open now, in a phase whose whole purpose is to find it, rather than
in Phase 4 when real screens are being built on top of it.

This plan also settles the platform-escape-hatch convention that roadmap success criterion 3 asks for, and
completes CONTEXT.md's native-module web audit — the discretionary item research called for so that feature
components in later phases do not pile onto an unverified pattern.

Purpose: PLAT-01 and success criteria 1 and 3; and the Profile screen finally mounts the PLAT-09 control and
the D-04 sign-out lifecycle that plans 01-02 and 01-05 built.
Output: a navigable app on three platforms, and a documented convention for handling the fourth divergence.
</objective>
