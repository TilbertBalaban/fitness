<objective>
Replace the generator's cycle-1-only volume and trim math with a hardest-cycle volume split and
session fit, and make the RIR ladder depend on `daysPerWeek`.

Purpose: three of the five reported root causes live here — a muscle group's whole weekly target
landing on one exercise (D-01/D-02), a trim that drops whole slots from the end on cycle-1 numbers
and never checks the last cycle (D-03/D-04), and a fixed RIR ladder that ignores training frequency
(D-09).

Output: two new pure modules (`volume-split.ts`, `session-fit.ts`), a rewritten day loop in
`generate.ts`, a `daysPerWeek`-keyed RIR ladder in `volume-landmarks.ts`, `session-length.ts`
reduced to the time model alone, and the existing suites brought back green against the replaced
behaviour.
</objective>
